## Proposal Writing

### Part A: Administrative Forms
- Consortium composition balance
- Budget distribution justification
- SME participation (if required)

### Part B: Technical Annex

#### Section 1: Excellence (Score: /5)

**1.1 Objectives**
```
Structure:
- Overall objective (project vision)
- Specific objectives (SO1, SO2, SO3...)
  - Measurable
  - Linked to work packages
  - SMART formulation
```

**1.2 Relation to Work Programme**
- Quote call text directly
- Map objectives to call requirements
- Demonstrate scope fit

**1.3 Concept and Methodology**
```
Structure:
1. Overall Concept
   - Innovation beyond state-of-the-art
   - Conceptual framework diagram

2. Scientific/Technical Quality
   - Methodology per objective
   - Interdisciplinary approach
   - Gender dimension in research content

3. State of the Art
   - Current landscape (cite recent sources)
   - Limitations and gaps
   - Positioning statement
```

**1.4 Ambition**
- TRL progression (start → end)
- Innovation potential
- Quantified targets

#### Section 2: Impact (Score: /5)

**2.1 Project's Pathways to Impact**
```
Pathway Template:
Outcome → [Target Group] → Impact

Expected Results:
- Result 1: [Description, measurable]
- Result 2: ...

Target Groups:
- Industry/SMEs
- Policy makers
- Research community
- Civil society
```

**2.2 Measures to Maximize Impact**
```
Dissemination Plan:
- Scientific (journals, conferences)
- Technical (workshops, demonstrators)
- Public (websites, social media, events)

Exploitation Plan:
- IPR management
- Business models
- Standardization activities
- Policy uptake strategy

Communication Plan:
- Key messages
- Target audiences
- Channels and activities
- Timeline
```

**2.3 Summary (Impact Table)**
| Expected Impact | KPI | Target | Measurement |
|-----------------|-----|--------|-------------|
| [Impact 1] | [Indicator] | [Value] | [Method] |

#### Section 3: Implementation (Score: /5)

**3.1 Work Plan**
```
WP Structure:
WP1: Project Management
WP2-N: Technical WPs
WPX: Dissemination, Exploitation, Communication

Per WP:
- Objectives
- Description of work (tasks)
- Deliverables (number, type, month)
- Milestones
- Lead beneficiary
- Person-months per partner
```

**3.2 Consortium**
- Partner competencies and roles
- Complementarity justification
- Geographic balance
- Third country justification if applicable

**3.3 Resources**
- Person-months distribution
- Budget justification
- Equipment costs
- Subcontracting rationale

## Deliverable Writing

### Structure
```
Document Information:
- Deliverable number: D[X.Y]
- Title:
- Lead beneficiary:
- Due date: M[X]
- Nature: R (Report) / DEM / DEC / DATA / OTHER
- Dissemination: PU / SEN / CI

Executive Summary (1 page)

1. Introduction
   - Deliverable context within project
   - Objectives addressed
   - Relationship to other deliverables

2. [Technical Content Sections]
   - Methodology
   - Results
   - Analysis

3. Conclusions
   - Key findings
   - Relation to project objectives
   - Input to other WPs

References
Annexes
```

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Addresses stated objectives
- [ ] Consistent with DoA
- [ ] Proper EC acknowledgment
- [ ] Disclaimer included
- [ ] Version control
- [ ] Partner contributions attributed

## Periodic Reporting

### Technical Report Structure
```
1. Explanation of Work (per WP)
   - Objectives for period
   - Progress and achievements
   - Deviations and corrective actions
   - Use of resources

2. Milestones and Deliverables
   - Milestone status
   - Deliverable status
   - Explanation of delays

3. Critical Implementation Risks
   - Risk identification
   - Mitigation measures
   - Impact assessment

4. Impact
   - Dissemination activities
   - Communication activities
   - Exploitation progress
```

### Financial Report Alignment
- Person-months reported vs planned
- Budget categories
- Explanation of transfers
- Unforeseen costs

## Key Success Factors

**Excellence:**
- Clear SotA positioning with citations
- Measurable objectives
- Sound methodology with justification
- Appropriate ambition level

**Impact:**
- Concrete pathways, not vague claims
- Quantified KPIs
- Stakeholder engagement evidence
- Business model clarity

**Implementation:**
- Realistic timeline with contingency
- Clear partner roles
- Appropriate resources
- Risk management plan

## Output Format

For proposals: Section-by-section feedback with scoring prediction
For deliverables: Structure review and content completeness
