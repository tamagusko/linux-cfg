# Faculty Job Campaign

Make the candidate's case clear, credible, and relevant to the advertised job.
Preserve the Claude skill's four modes and 22 numbered principles, with
role-specific evidence and realistic uncertainty replacing universal hiring claims.

## Start with the actual task

Inspect the supplied CV, advertisement, draft, and relevant repository instructions.
Reuse existing candidate records, application templates, and document generators.
Ask only for missing facts that change the decision: target role or advertisement,
country, deadline, and available time. Continue independent work while waiting.
For an exploratory request without a named post, state a provisional target;
do not invent institutional criteria or predict an actual shortlist.

| Mode | Use when | Read next | Default deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnose | Assess competitiveness, gaps, or application fit | [Diagnosis and positioning](references/diagnose-position.md) | Initial read, criteria/evidence table, objection audit, bounded verdict |
| Position | Define research identity or a coherent case | [Diagnosis and positioning](references/diagnose-position.md) | Identity line, evidence, alternatives, department fit |
| Plan | Prioritise actions before applications | [Campaign playbook](references/playbook.md) | Time-budgeted actions, dependencies, deadlines, expected benefit |
| Review | Improve a supplied campaign asset | [Review rubrics](references/review-rubrics.md) | Readiness verdict, ranked fixes, replacement wording |

Run only the requested modes. Combine them when the decision needs it, without
forcing a full diagnosis before a narrow edit. Read the relevant numbered entries
in [Principles](references/principles.md), not every supporting file by default.
Use the default formats unless the user or application specifies another format.

## Evidence discipline

- Separate **documented**, **not documented**, **inferred**, and **needs verification**.
  An omission from a CV does not establish that the experience is absent.
- Tie findings to a file section/page or an official criterion. Label expectations
  based on judgment; do not invent publication thresholds or institution tiers.
- Assess research, teaching, supervision, service, and impact in the proportions
  the role actually requests. Funding is not a universal primary criterion.
- Preserve publication status, grant role, award versus application, supervision
  role, and shared contributions. Do not infer independence solely from author
  position or absence of a supervisor; conventions vary by discipline.
- Verify current vacancies, deadlines and time zones, funding calls, and eligibility
  against official sources when they affect advice. Record the URL and check date.
  If unavailable, mark the decision provisional and specify what needs checking.
- Do not infer nationality, work permission, disability, family circumstances,
  or willingness to relocate. Ask only when material. Report legal requirements
  from current official sources; do not determine eligibility from a name or CV.
- Distinguish credible positioning from unsupported claims of being first, best,
  or uniquely qualified. Retain honest scientific uncertainty and career context.
- Examples in this skill are patterns, never evidence about the current candidate.

## Scope and execution

Support strategy, drafting, and review. When asked to produce documents, follow
local generators and the advertisement's file/page requirements; use an available
formatting skill when needed. Manuscript review and journal selection belong to
their specialist workflows.

A campaign plan or review does not authorize sending messages, contacting
referees or panel members, publishing posts, changing public profiles, or submitting
applications. Draft these locally unless the user explicitly requests the action.
Existing explicit authorization remains valid; do not ask for it again.
Respect advertised contact channels and conflict-of-interest restrictions.

Use tools actually available in the session. Do not assume Claude commands,
named agents, or connectors exist. Delegate only a useful, bounded pass under the
session's routing instructions. A failed independent review is not approval.

## Writing and completion

Write in English with concrete evidence and restrained, committee-credible prose.
Avoid hype, stock AI phrasing, and em dashes in newly drafted candidate prose.
Preserve the user's voice, quotations, and official wording where required.
Offer specific rewrites rather than criticism alone. Do not manufacture praise,
a blocker, a rejected option, or a skipped action to fill a template.

End with the finding or deliverable, remaining evidence gaps, and the next useful
action. If files were requested, save them in the established campaign location
and link them. Never label an application submitted or independently approved
without evidence of that event.
